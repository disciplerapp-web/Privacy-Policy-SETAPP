Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Set. mobile application ("Set.", "the App").

Set. is a workout tracking application designed to make strength training easier to track. The App allows users to create an account, log workouts, track exercises and lifts, record repetitions and weights, and monitor personal records and progress over time.

1. Data Controller

Set. is operated by:

Clearlane Development AB
Sweden
Email: discipler.app@gmail.com

Clearlane Development AB is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with the App.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Set., we may collect information that you provide directly to us or that is generated through your use of the App.

This may include:

  • Email address and account information
  • Authentication and login information
  • Profile information that you choose to provide
  • Workout history
  • Exercises performed
  • Weights and repetitions
  • Personal records (PRs)
  • Estimated one-repetition maximums (1RM)
  • Workout dates and times
  • Workout and strength progression
  • Other information you choose to enter into the App

We collect this information to provide the core functionality of Set., including saving your workouts, synchronizing your account, tracking your progress, and displaying your training history.

3. Account and Login Information

Set. requires users to create or sign in to an account in order to use account-based features of the App.

Depending on the authentication method you use, Set. may process information such as your email address, authentication identifier, and account credentials or authentication tokens.

Authentication information is used to securely identify your account and allow you to access your workout data across supported devices.

We do not use your login information for advertising purposes.

4. Workout and Strength Data

Set. stores information about your workouts so that you can review your training history and track your strength progression.

This may include exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout dates, personal records, estimated 1RM values, and other workout information that you enter or generate while using the App.

Your workout data is associated with your Set. account so that it can be saved and made available to you when you use the App.

5. Voice-Based Workout Tracking

Set. may allow you to use your voice to describe or log a workout set. Voice input is used to interpret information such as the exercise performed, weight, repetitions, or other workout details.

Voice input may be processed by the technologies and services required to convert your spoken input into workout information.

Set. uses voice input to make workout logging faster and more convenient. Voice input is used for workout tracking and is not intended for advertising or behavioral profiling.

If voice recordings are temporarily processed or stored by a third-party speech or AI service, such processing is performed according to the applicable service provider's terms and privacy practices.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through Set. for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining your account
  • Authenticating users
  • Saving and displaying your workouts
  • Tracking exercises, weights, and repetitions
  • Recording and identifying personal records
  • Calculating or displaying estimated 1RM values
  • Showing strength and workout progression over time
  • Providing voice-based workout logging
  • Maintaining and improving the App
  • Providing customer support
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the App
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

Account and workout information may be stored on servers or cloud infrastructure used to operate Set.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

These providers are only permitted to process information as necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

10. Advertising

11. Sharing of Information
